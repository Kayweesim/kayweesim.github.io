Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Story Studio is a personal tool that runs on its owner's own computer and uploads videos to the owner's own YouTube channel. This policy explains what data it accesses and what it does with it.

YouTube API Services

Story Studio uses YouTube API Services. By using Story Studio you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Data accessed through YouTube API Services is also covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

What data it accesses

When you connect a YouTube channel, Story Studio asks Google for permission to:

  • Upload videos to the connected channel (youtube.upload), and
  • View the account (youtube.readonly), used only to show the channel's name and link, and to check the processing and privacy status of videos Story Studio uploaded.

It does not access email, contacts, other videos, comments, subscribers, analytics or payment information.

How the data is used

The data is used only to upload videos that the owner has made and manually approved, and to show whether those uploads succeeded. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Where the data is stored

Story Studio has no server or cloud database. Everything stays on the owner's computer:

  • the Google sign-in token, in a local file readable only by the owner's user account;
  • the channel's name and link; and
  • for each uploaded video, its YouTube video ID, link and upload status.

Video files and their titles, descriptions and tags are sent to YouTube only when an upload is approved.

Sharing

Story Studio does not sell, share or transfer any user data to anyone. The only party it sends data to is Google/YouTube, to perform the uploads requested.

Cookies

Story Studio does not place cookies or tracking technologies in your browser or on your device, and this website has no analytics or trackers.

Removing access and deleting data

  • Revoke Story Studio's access to your Google account at any time from Google security settings.
  • Choosing Disconnect in Story Studio deletes the stored sign-in token from the computer immediately.
  • To have any other stored data deleted, email the address below; it will be deleted within 7 days.

The upload status shown for each video is fetched fresh from YouTube each time Story Studio's Uploads page is opened.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will be updated.
